Travel information

Airport

The main airport serving Cagliari is Cagliari-Elmas (IATA code: CAG), located about 7 km from the city center. It is connected to many Italian and European cities by regular flights operated by several airlines, including low-cost carriers. More information about flights to Cagliari can be found on the airport website: Cagliari Airport.

Taxi

Taxis are available outside the arrivals terminal at Cagliari-Elmas airport. The trip to the city center takes about 15 minutes and costs approximately 20-25 Euros.

There is no single company for taxi in Cagliari. An easy option is the appTaxi app for smartphones.

Public transportation

The most convenient connection between the Cagliari-Elmas airport and the center of Cagliari is provided by a train leaving every 30 minutes from the Airport and reaching Piazza Matteotti. The trip lasts less than 10 minutes and the ticket is inexpensive. Hotel Regina Margherita is 700m from the square and can be reached by feet or by using the bus n. 7.

Climate & Dressing

October in Sardinia is the start of fall with mild weather of approximately 20-24°C in the daytime and about 14-18 °C in the evenings. We recommend you to bring layered clothing and rain coats for possible showers.

Electricity Supply

The standard electrical voltage in Italy is 220 volts AC, 50Hz with European type connections. (L and F here)
