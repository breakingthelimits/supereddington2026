In this Page we will publish the travel information.

Travel information

By private car

By public transportation

The most convenient connection between the Cagliari-Elmas airport and the center of Cagliari is provided by a train leaving every 30 minutes from the Airport and reaching Piazza Matteotti. The trip lasts less than 10 minutes and the ticket is inexpensive. Hotel Regina Margherita is 700m from the square and can be reached by feet or by using the bus n. 7.

Participants are advised to obtain their own travel insurance policies. Attendees waive any claim against the Organization for injury or other damages resulting in any way from Conference participation.

Climate & Dressing

October in Sardinia is the start of fall with mild weather of approximately 20-24°C in the daytime and about 14-18 °C in the evenings. We recommend you to bring layered clothing and rain coats for possible showers.

Electricity Supply

The standard electrical voltage in Italy is 220 volts AC, 50Hz with European type connections. (L and F here)

Badges

Wearing badges is mandatory in order to have an access to the scientific sessions, refreshments and social events.

Smoking Policy

The Organization Committee has a no-smoking policy during all scientific sessions. In Italy, smoking is prohibited in all closed areas.

Certificate of Attendance

A certificate of attendance will be given to all registered participants.
